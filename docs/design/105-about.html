What we log, and why

Every screen in this app fires a real event at a hosted database, so there's an actual aggregate to look at, not a simulated one. Here's exactly what that means, event family by event family:

  • A random ID for this browser, generated the first time you do anything here and stored only in this browser. It isn't your name, email, or anything that identifies you — it's a made-up number that lets us tell "one visitor came back" from "two different visitors," nothing more.
  • Which city you're browsing, and when you switch — location_selected, fired only the first time you pick a city or when you pick a different one than the city already remembered for this browser, never on every visit.
  • Which screens you visit and when — the home feed (home_viewed), a restaurant's menu (restaurant_opened), your cart (cart_viewed), checkout (checkout_viewed), and the tracker, including every time you come back to check on an order (tracker_viewed).
  • The flash-deal sheet that sometimes opens on the home feed — flash_sheet_shown and flash_sheet_closed.
  • The choices you make at checkout — your drop-off preset, your delivery instructions, whether you want utensils, and which real voucher or vouchers, if any, you applied (order_placed). There is no name, address, phone number, email, or payment field anywhere in this app for us to log.
  • Whether your order actually arrives, and how you rated it — order_delivered and rating_submitted.

What we don't log: anything that identifies you as a real person. No account, no card, no email, no address, no cross-site tracking cookie, and no free-text field anywhere in this app for any of the above, ever.

One more thing, in the interest of actually being honest about it: to stop spam from overwhelming this free database, every write is checked against a short-term limit keyed to a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of your IP address, kept for up to one hour, only to enforce that limit — it never appears next to anything you did here.
